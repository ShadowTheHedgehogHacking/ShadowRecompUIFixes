## What does this do?
* Fixes the seams on the mission and command indicators
* Higher res and fixed alignment numbers (same as the one we shipped in Dolphin Addons for Reloaded)

## Note
* This does fully replace the affected xncps and the TXDs in pause, as we didn't add replace.ini support for those. If you're making a mod editing those files feel free to take out the edits and repack them into your mod. Maybe something to change in v1.1.0 of ShadowRecomp later.
